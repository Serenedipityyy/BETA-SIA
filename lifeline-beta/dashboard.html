🩹 Campus First Aid Basics

  • Dizziness / Fainting: Sit down immediately and lower head between knees or lie down with feet elevated.
  • Bleeding: Apply direct, firm pressure to the wound with a clean cloth.
  • Asthma / Breathing: Stay calm, sit upright, and notify CPR medics for oxygen support.
  • Heat Exhaustion: Move to a shaded area and sip water slowly.
